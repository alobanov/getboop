A private diary app with no account

Boop Diary opens straight to today’s page. There is no sign-up, no email and no password: you just start writing.

No account, nothing to sign up for

Boop Diary has no accounts at all. You install it, pick how your pen and paper look, and today’s page is ready. With no account there is no server holding your diary and no profile for anyone to read, the developer included.

Your entries stay on your phone

Everything you put in a day, the note, the photos, the sketches, the mood and the habits, is saved by itself as you go and stays on your phone.

If you want a spare copy, Boop Diary can keep one in your own iCloud Drive. It saves there whenever you leave the app, and on a new phone it offers to bring the diary back. It is a spare copy, not a sync between devices.

You can also export a backup file, a zip with your entries and photos, and restore it later on any phone.

A lock of its own

A code you choose and Face ID can close the diary on the phone itself, at once or after 1, 5 or 15 minutes away.

What the app does send

Boop Diary sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, which help find what breaks. What you write is never sent, and you can turn them off in Settings. The details are in the privacy policy.

Made by one person

Boop Diary is made by one developer, not a studio: no ads, no investors. If something is missing, write to the developer. Every letter gets read.
