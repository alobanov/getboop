A page for every day.

Write what happened, pick a mood, drop in photos. Boop gives the day a doodle of its own, and you can swap it for any of two hundred others.

  • Dotted, lined, squared or plain paper
  • Photos in a pile, sketches beside the words
  • A mood for the day, in its own color

02

Trace a photo, keep the sketch.

Open any photo of the day under the pen and draw over it. The photo goes, the drawing stays on the page.

  • A pen, an eraser, undo and redo
  • Up to six sketches a day
  • Each sketch laid out beside the note, large or small

03

Habits that color your days.

Give each habit a color. Do it, and the day’s doodle takes it on, so the year shows at a glance what you kept up.

  • Every day, on set weekdays, or 3 times a month, whenever it suits
  • A tick, a count, minutes or a checklist
  • Bad habits get a limit, and going over it shows
