One drawing a day

Write a line, pick a mood, add a photo. The day gets its own doodle from a set of 160, and you can swap it for another.

What you do adds color

Every habit has a color: a run, water, reading, less coffee. Check one off and the day’s doodle takes that color.

Your year, drawn

The canvas grows one day at a time. No streaks and no pressure, just a year you can look back at.

It stays on your phone

No account, no servers, no ads, no analytics. Your entries and photos live on your device and nowhere else.

Moving to a new phone? Export a backup file in Settings and restore it there. Read the privacy policy.
