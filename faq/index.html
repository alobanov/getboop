Boop Diary: questions, answered

Short answers about Boop Diary, the diary for iPhone and iPad where every day you write becomes a small drawing.

Is there a diary app without sign up?

Yes: Boop Diary for iPhone and iPad has no account and no sign-up. You open it and start writing on today’s page. Your entries are saved by themselves and stay on your phone.

Where does Boop Diary keep my entries?

On your phone. If you turn on the iCloud copy, Boop Diary also saves a spare copy to your own iCloud Drive whenever you leave the app. That copy is a backup, not a sync between devices.

Can anyone else read my diary?

Not through Boop Diary: there is no account and no server holding your entries, so nobody else can read them, the developer included. You can also close the diary with a code and Face ID. The app sends only anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, never what you write, and you can turn them off in Settings.

Can I turn photos into drawings in a journal?

Yes. In Boop Diary, Autosketch draws a photo for you on a day’s sketch sheet, with a pen or in crayon. Then you can draw over it, rub out what you don’t need and keep the sketch on that day.

Does Autosketch send my photos anywhere?

No. The drawing is made on your iPhone or iPad itself, and the photo is not uploaded anywhere.

Does Boop Diary work on iPad?

Yes. Boop Diary runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. For now your iPhone and your iPad each keep a diary of their own and don’t sync with each other. There is no Android version yet.

Can I track habits and mood in the same journal?

Yes. In Boop Diary you can tick a habit off, count it, time it in minutes or keep it as a checklist, give a habit you’re quitting a limit, and bring in your steps from Apple Health. Each day’s mood is one tap on one of 15 faces. The habits you mark fill that day’s doodle with their colours.

How do I move my diary to a new phone?

With the iCloud copy on, install Boop Diary on the new phone and it offers to bring your diary back. Without it, choose Export backup in Settings, keep the zip file, and choose Restore from backup on the new phone. Restoring replaces everything in the app with the backup.

Can I lock my diary with Face ID?

Yes. Boop Diary can close your diary with a code you choose and with Face ID. It locks at once or after 1, 5 or 15 minutes away.

Can I see a whole month at a glance?

Yes. When a month ends, Boop Diary gathers it into one picture, the month’s doodles, sketches or photos day by day, ready to share. A single day can also become a 4:5 poster in one of five styles.

What languages does Boop Diary speak?

English, Russian, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Serbian, Japanese, Korean and Traditional Chinese. It follows your phone’s language, and you can pick another one for the app in the iPhone’s Settings.

Who makes Boop Diary?

One developer, Aleksei Lobanov, not a studio. There are no ads and no investors. The developer reads every letter.
