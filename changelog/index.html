What’s new

1.0.12

November 2, 2026

Boop has a new face and a whole shelf of icons.

New icon

The notebook is now a felt sticker on yellow, waving at you from the home screen. In your phone's dark mode it moves onto a dark background by itself.

Icons in tiles

Icons now sit in tiles, in two groups: Boop and the margin dwellers.

Everything else in 1.0.12

  • NewGlossy is redrawn, and Pencil and Dark are new. Dark stays dark all the time.

1.0.11

October 31, 2026

Ready looks, a sheet torn from a notebook and your year by month.

Ready looks

In Appearance, paper, ink, accent, ruling and font change with one tap. Three looks are free, six more come with Boop Plus. A look's accent now shows on the date, the wavy line and the buttons.

Sheet edge and details

The day's sheet can be torn out of a notebook, turn into a postage stamp or crumple softly. Add a dog-ear, punched holes and washi tape holding your photo. Each day tears and sticks a little differently. In Boop Plus.

By month, and slip days

Statistics now have a bar for each month and a line saying whether the last one went better or worse. For a habit you're quitting you can see what slip days were like: mood, weather and the last three slips. In Boop Plus.

Everything else in 1.0.11

  • NewA new font in Boop Plus: Pencil, plain and a little childlike.
  • BetterThe next day comes in whole the moment you swipe.
  • BetterA habit you're quitting stands out in statistics: a red cross on its chip, slips in red, days with no entry in grey.
  • BetterMood blots have a thin edge, so the pale ones no longer sink into the paper.
  • FixedFor habits with a limit, clean days are no longer blank, and an allowed time no longer looks like a slip.

1.0.10

October 29, 2026

The pot has come alive: it sneezes, whistles, chats and has visitors.

The pot comes alive

While you watch, it sneezes so hard a leaf falls off, gets startled, sniffs a flower or whistles to itself. Run a finger over it and it giggles. And its bubble says something new each time.

Visitors and a shelf

A butterfly, a bee and a ladybug come to the flowers. Your streak's milestones put a watering can, books and a little heart frame on the shelf, and they stay even if the streak breaks. On a day a new flower opens, bunting hangs under the shelf.

Everything else in 1.0.10

  • NewUnder the pot's heading there's now your week, with the streak's days ticked.
  • BetterThe bush grows once a day and then stands in bloom. To watch it grow again, tap the button under the pot shapes.
  • BetterBoop opens faster and uses less memory.
  • BetterWhile a page loads, the mood faces hop instead of a spinning wheel.
  • FixedWhen your phone turns down one reminder, the days after it no longer lose theirs.

1.0.9

October 27, 2026

Pick the paper for your days: its colour, crumpled folds or kraft.

Your own paper for the day

Appearance now has «Sheet colour» and «Texture». In the light theme the sheet can be milk, cream or white, in the dark one milk or grey — or keep it matching the background, as before. On top: crumpled paper, kraft or nothing. If you already had crumpled paper on, it stays.

Clear a day

A day's page now has a bin at the top right. It asks first and shows what will go: the mood, habit check-ins, the note, photos and sketches. This can't be undone.

Everything else in 1.0.9

  • BetterA tap leaves a light wash of ink, and a habit you drag lifts on a scrap of paper and lands in its new place right away.

1.0.8

October 26, 2026

Your streak now grows in a little pot — a leaf for every day.

Your streak grows in a pot

Left of the year there's a button with a sprout and your days in a row. Tap it and a bush grows out of the pot: a leaf for every day you write, and a daisy, a tulip, a poppy and a sunflower bloom on days 3, 7, 14 and 30. Missing a day ruins nothing: the old streak stays beside it in pencil.

The pot is alive

It cheers for every flower, blinks and gets up to mischief — and if you poke it, it says “Boooop!”. Pick its shape: round, classic, a tub or a vase.

New faces for your moods

15 lively faces instead of the old ones, each a blot of its own colour you read at a glance. They blink, hop, droop and fume, and the mood you pick answers right away. The widget has the same faces.

Everything else in 1.0.8

  • BetterThe day's sheet now fills the page: the mood at the bottom, habits above it, and a tap on empty paper starts a note.
  • BetterThe settings button on the year screen now shows sliders.

1.0.7

October 23, 2026

Your month now fits in one picture, and the reminder pays more attention.

Month recap

When a month ends, a banner up top says «October is done», with how it went and a «Share the month» button: the whole month in one picture — doodles, sketches or photos day by day, your favourite drawings and your best streak. Easy to post to your feed. Any month's recap is also in the menu under its name in the month view.

A reminder that guards your streak

If the day is already written, there's no reminder. If not, Boop tells you how many days in a row you've kept and whether someone might turn up in the margins today.

Everything else in 1.0.7

  • BetterBefore the plans, a table: what's free and what Plus adds.
  • Fixed«Draw it for me» with the crayon no longer closes the app.

1.0.6

October 20, 2026

Your habits can now be counted up, and the canvas has a crayon.

Habit statistics

The chart button above the year shows your days as squares — over a month, three or the whole year — with the share of your plan done, the rhythm of your week with its weak day, and your records. With Boop Plus, each habit's streaks and how it goes with your mood.

A crayon on the canvas

Next to the pen and the eraser there's a crayon in nine colours, with a waxy grain. Colour in a sketch or draw the whole thing with it.

The year answers faster

The date above your finger and the trail across the days appear after a quarter of a second, or at once if you slide. A short tap simply opens the day.

Autosketch

The photo button on the canvas is now “Autosketch”: a vase shows what the pen will draw and what the crayon will. The tools under the sheet have labels, and the first time you open the canvas, notes show what's where.

Everything else in 1.0.6

  • NewSave a sketch to Photos as a transparent picture, from its menu on the day's page.
  • NewFuture days show doodles too when you slide your finger over them.
  • BetterPlacing a sketch is clearer: the page layout shows the sketches themselves, and a taken spot clears as you watch.
  • BetterThe eraser rubs out crayon as well, including crayon drawings made from photos.
  • Better“Done” lights up only once something is drawn, and “Clear” asks first.
  • BetterCamera or gallery now come as big cards, and sheets have a round close button.
  • FixedThe Wild Pickle dives into the portal without a white box around him.

1.0.5

October 18, 2026

Photos can now be drawn in crayon, and a sketch takes up exactly as much room as its drawing.

Crayon sketches from photos

“Draw it for me” now has two ways: “With a pen” and “In crayon”. The crayon lays the photo out in patches of colour, finds clothes, hands and even straps, and puts the drawing under the canvas. You can draw over it with the pen.

Framed right on the canvas

A photo from the camera or the gallery can be moved, zoomed and turned. The square you set is what gets drawn, not the middle of the shot.

Something to watch while it draws

A paper veil covers the canvas and a scribble draws itself on it. Changed your mind? “Cancel” brings the photo back and stops the work.

Sketches cut to their drawing

New sketches are trimmed to what is drawn, and the text goes right under them, no empty margin. Older ones stay square until they are saved again.

Everything else in 1.0.5

  • NewCrayon sketches show in the day’s post, on the month’s covers, in the list and in the widget.
  • NewBoop now cheers you at the halfway mark of your steps goal too, not only at three quarters and at the goal.
  • NewThis very screen: what’s new in each version. Always at hand in Settings.
  • BetterA photo on the canvas can be zoomed out smaller than the square, so a bigger thing fits.
  • BetterSketches on the day’s page stand nearly at the paper’s edge, while the text keeps its margin.
  • FixedSteps notifications are no longer lost while the phone is locked, and their switch is honestly off when notifications aren’t allowed.
  • FixedThe phone no longer turns into a hand warmer after an update: the drawing models get ready once per version, one at a time, unhurried.
  • FixedIn Kyiv, Amsterdam, Stockholm and 129 more time zones the reminder came by UTC, in Kyiv two or three hours late. Now it’s on time.

1.0.4

October 17, 2026

Steps remind you of the goal, the date no longer jumps while you swipe, and the weather shows the whole day.

Steps nudges

At three quarters of the day’s goal and when it’s reached, Boop sends a notification, each at most once a day. Turn them off in Settings, under “Steps nudges”.

The date stays calm

Swiping through days, the date and weather glide off with the swipe and the next one slides in. The week’s dots no longer jump either.

Weather, low to high

The date now shows “+8…+15°” instead of the high alone. The icon sits centred on the numbers in any font.

Camera off? Boop says where

If camera access is off, a note with “Open Settings” shows up instead of silence. And the gallery no longer asks for photo access: it doesn’t need it.

Everything else in 1.0.4

  • BetterThe system’s questions about the camera, photos, Health, location and Face ID now speak the phone’s language, not always English.
  • Better“Done” in a sketch is now a green circle with a tick. It used to pass for a checkbox.
  • BetterTyping and swiping through days are smoother, and the iCloud copy no longer slows the launch.
  • BetterSun dust shows on the light theme too, and the lightning flash is softer.
  • FixedGoing through the introduction again no longer makes a second “Walking” habit.
  • FixedDay post: in a short iPad window “Share” can always be scrolled to, the mood drawing makes it into the picture, and each language gets its own quote marks.
  • FixedIf a photo for a sketch fails to come in, Boop says so instead of closing.

1.0.3

October 16, 2026

A day can become a poster, the weather on the page came alive, and steps no longer take a free spot.

Share the day

A new item in the day’s plus turns the day into a 4:5 poster for your feed. Five styles: “Cover”, “Zine”, “Doodles”, “Strips” and “Clippings”. Free for everyone.

Livelier weather on the page

Lightning strikes in a storm, drizzle is finally visible, small flakes swirl over the snow, and clouds drawn in pen drift slowly across the paper.

Steps don’t count

Without Boop Plus you get three habits, plus steps from Health on top. Steps no longer go to the archive or open the paywall.

Boop on iPhone Duo

The home screen, the introduction and the paywall fill the whole screen, and the buttons sit in the system strip under the clock.

Everything else in 1.0.3

  • NewThe poster can be saved to Photos right from the share sheet.
  • BetterThe introduction replayed from Settings opens full screen, not in the settings window.
  • FixedTyping a number into the habits table in a wide window no longer hangs on a dimmed screen.

1.0.2

October 2026

Boop comes to iPad, with the weather of the day and steps as real places.

Boop on iPad

Lay your diary open like a notebook: the year or the month on the left, the day on the right. Settings, habit editing and sketching open in a card in the middle, and Boop works in Split View and Stage Manager. Syncing with iPhone comes next.

The weather of the day

A small drawn weather mark and the day’s high beside the date, for today and the two days before. Tap it to pick the weather yourself. With Plus the weather plays over the page: rain, snow, fog, a storm, wind and sun.

Steps as real places

Under your steps: about three times Charles Bridge. Over the habits: the month’s walk, from Paris to London. Forty places known the world over, each with its own sticker and one true fact.

A start, step by step

The introduction now sets Boop up with you, one step at a time, each with its own preview: empty days, theme, handwriting and paper. Then your first day opens, with a note in pen to write a few words.

Everything else in 1.0.2

  • NewDone in the day’s header while you type, then a pale Saved over the page, so you know it’s kept.
  • NewBoth dotted papers, Dots and Dust, are free.
  • NewEvery dialog is a note on your paper, a little askew, with a strip of tape on top.
  • NewLandscapes in pen at the foot of Settings and of the inhabitants’ page.
  • NewAn About Boop page: who makes it, and why there are no accounts and no ads.
  • NewNew installs start with crumpled paper, doodles on empty days and espresso ink.
  • BetterThe buy button on the Boop Plus page stays in sight on the smallest iPhones and with display zoom.
  • BetterPlus switches in Appearance wear a crown; what you pick is kept and turns on with Plus.

1.0.1

October 2026

Draw for me, a lock for your diary and the diary as a list.

Draw for me

Under a photo on the sketch canvas, tap Draw for me and watch the phone draw the photo in Boop’s pen, line by line, as if by hand. One Undo takes it all back. It runs on your phone: the photo goes nowhere.

A lock for your diary

Your own four-digit code, and Face ID. A closed diary is a hand-drawn lock on paper, in the app switcher too. It locks at once, or after one, five or fifteen minutes.

The diary as a list

A third view beside the year and the month: the year’s days, newest first, each with its note, doodle, habits and a photo or sketch. Tap a day to open it.

Steps from Apple Health

A habit that fills itself: steps come in from Health, with a goal for the day. Past days arrive at once. Boop reads only your steps, and keeps and sends nothing.

1.0

October 2026

The first Boop. A diary and a habit tracker that draws your year as you live it.

What was in it

  • A day: a few lines, a mood, photos, a sketch traced over a photo, and a doodle of its own.
  • Habits in color: tick one, and the day’s doodle takes it on.
  • The year and the month on one page.
  • Inhabitants of the margins: characters who move in as you keep writing, each with a page of field notes.
  • Six widgets for the Home Screen.
  • Boop Plus, by the week, month or year, or once for good: as many habits as you like, a copy in your iCloud, the widgets, every font and paper.
  • No account, no ads; everything stays on your phone.
  • Seven languages: English, Russian, German, Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese.
