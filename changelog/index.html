What’s new

Every version of Boop, the newest first.

1.0.1

LatestOctober 2026

Draw for mePlus · 3 free

Under a photo on the sketch canvas, tap Draw for me and watch the phone draw the photo in Boop’s pen, line by line, as if by hand. One Undo takes it all back. It runs on your phone: the photo goes nowhere.

A lock for your diaryFree

Your own four-digit code, and Face ID. A closed diary is a hand-drawn lock on paper, in the app switcher too. It locks at once, or after one, five or fifteen minutes.

The diary as a list

A third view beside the year and the month: the year’s days, newest first, each with its note, doodle, habits and a photo or sketch. Tap a day to open it.

Steps from Apple HealthPlus

A habit that fills itself: steps come in from Health, with a goal for the day. Past days arrive at once. Boop reads only your steps, and keeps and sends nothing.

New

  • Boop speaks Serbian, in the Latin script, with the inhabitants named anew.
  • Ink splashes fly from under the mood stamp as it lands on the page.
  • Tips on first launch: notes in the margins, with arrows, showing where things are.
  • Tap the month’s name to pick what the days show first: photos, sketches or doodles.
  • Pick which inhabitants walk around your diary, on each one’s page.
  • A warm cream paper and an espresso ink in Appearance.
  • The Language row in Settings opens Boop’s page in iPhone Settings.

Improved

  • The inhabitants have new names and new jokes, in every language.
  • Their pages proofread again in German, Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese.
  • Larger photos in the month, and a pile that shows it’s a pile.
  • A day’s number in the month takes the color of its doodle.
  • The photo’s sliders in a sketch respond along the whole row.
  • Empty days are drawn in your own ink color.
  • The Day tab’s icon is a tear-off calendar.

Fixed

  • The reminder comes in the app’s language.
  • Words agree with their numbers in Russian and Serbian: 5 записей, 21 запись.
  • A habit held to be dragged no longer turns into an empty block.
  • A diary written by a newer Boop asks you to update instead of failing.
  • Prices are rounded to the currency, and a missing price shows a dash, not endless dots.

1.0

October 2026

The first Boop. A diary and a habit tracker that draws your year as you live it.

In it

  • A day: a few lines, a mood, photos, a sketch traced over a photo, and a doodle of its own.
  • Habits in color: tick one, and the day’s doodle takes it on.
  • The year and the month on one page.
  • Inhabitants of the margins: characters who move in as you keep writing, each with a page of field notes.
  • Six widgets for the Home Screen.
  • Boop Plus, by the week, month or year, or once for good: as many habits as you like, a copy in your iCloud, the widgets, every font and paper.
  • No account, no ads; everything stays on your phone.
  • Seven languages: English, Russian, German, Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese.
